# Qais Zakaria (Zak)

## Summary
Qais Zakaria (referred to throughout the Nomad letters as "Zak") is the co-manager of the [[Nomad_Investment_Partnership]] alongside [[Nick_Sleep]]. He is a co-founder of Sleep, Zakaria and Company, Ltd., the investment advisory firm that managed Nomad throughout its 2001–2014 life.

## Explanation

### Role at Nomad
Zakaria co-authored all Nomad letters to Partners and shared equal responsibility for portfolio construction, research, and the fund's investment philosophy. The letters are primarily written in Sleep's voice but consistently refer to "Zak and I" as a unified decision-making unit. No significant distinction between their individual roles or views is drawn in the letters.

### Post-Nomad
On closing Nomad in 2014, Sleep wrote that Zak was pursuing "various charitable causes." In the 2021 preamble to the IGY Foundation edition of the letters, Sleep and Zakaria jointly authored the opening note, reaffirming their shared view that charitable giving is a natural and meaningful destination for investment success.

### Quotes
> "After thirteen happy years of running the Nomad Investment Partnership, Zak and I have decided to close the fund." — Nick Sleep, letter to Warren Buffett, June 3, 2014
