## Investment Concepts & Mental Models

- [[Absolute_Return_Orientation]] — beating the index as a byproduct, not the goal
- [[Margin_of_Safety]] — buying dollar bills for fifty cents; intrinsic value methods; recycling from 65c to new 50c
- [[Owner_Oriented_Management]] — management that thinks and acts like long-term owners
- [[Franchise_Businesses]] — "difficult to copy" competitive moats
- [[Portfolio_Classification_Framework]] — franchises / asset-backed / deep value buckets
- [[Short_Termism]] — the dominant market dysfunction that creates mispricings
- [[Patience_as_Competitive_Advantage]] — investor base patience as edge; "never mark emotions to market"
- [[Per_Share_Discipline]] — share count stability as a screen for corporate character
- [[Diworsification]] — value-destroying diversification (Peter Lynch term)
- [[Accounting_Manipulations]] — SFAS 13, earnings management, cash flow as true anchor
- [[Expected_Returns_Model]] — 50c entry + 10% growth = 26% p.a.; two prime determinants (Letter 5)
- [[Terminal_Portfolio]] — 15 "super high-quality thinkers"; want to vs have to hold (Letter 6)
- [[Kelly_Criterion]] — optimal bet sizing; Nomad has been too diversified (Letter 6)
- [[Portfolio_Concentration]] — three harms of over-diversification; governance dividend (Letter 6)
- [[Active_Ownership]] — >10% stakes enabling real shareholder influence (Letter 6)
- [[Cognitive_Biases_in_Investing]] — attribution error; loss aversion 3× asymmetry; commitment bias; limbic override; social proof; availability heuristic (Letters 5, 7, 8, 9)
- [[Scale_Economies_Shared]] — the central investment model: share scale efficiencies with customers; moat grows as you give more back (Letters 7–8)
- [[Robustness_Ratio]] — customer savings ÷ shareholder earnings; Costco 5:1, GEICO 1:1; moat measurement (Letter 8)
- [[Valuation_Heuristics]] — P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA as intellectual laziness; growth/value false dichotomy (Letter 7)
- [[Deep_Discount_to_Replacement_Cost]] — second primary investment model; Zimbabwe, Asian crisis, Union Cement (Letter 8)
- [[Probability_Based_Thinking]] — Zeckhauser bridge analogy; decision trees; Monty Hall; four psychological misjudgements (Letter 9 / speech appendix)
- [[Principal_Agent_Conflict]] — root cause of industry dysfunction; refundable performance fee; cost-reimbursement management fee; right to shrink (Letters 9–10)
- [[Capital_Cycle]] — high valuations attract supply; Thai cement; Phelps Dodge; anti-bubble in large-cap growth (Letters 9–10)
- [[Sleep_Zakaria_and_Company]] — formed March 2006; FSA authorised; full independence September 2006; 100% personal capital in Nomad (Letter 10)
- [[Equity_Yield_Curve]] — patience has measurable value; returns increase with holding period; Nomad cohort evidence; 20 vs 260 week holding gap (Letter 11)
- [[Scaling_Laws]] — Kleiber's Law; billion heartbeats; mouse-to-elephant skeletal structure; internet vs high street retail (Letter 12)
- [[Santa_Fe_Institute]] — interdisciplinary research; Complex Adaptive System; Gell-Mann creative thinking; new economics in waiting (Letters 11–12)
- [[Destination_Analysis]] — static view at purchase fails; evolving view of business destination; Conseco/Stagecoach; opportunity cost table; inversion tool (Letter 13)
- [[Mistakes_Framework]] — non-judgmental forgiveness; denial/anchoring/drift; MDC vs Bank X; NPV-positive mistakes; diversification as denial (Letter 13)
- [[Dilution_Risk]] — equity issuance below intrinsic value destroys per-share value; MBIA case; Wilbur Ross/Assured Guaranty solution (Letter 14)
- [[Longevity_of_Compound]] — Wal-Mart cheap for decades; three reasons investors miss great companies (Barbie Problem, structural/behavioural, odds); monopoly of long-term investing (Letter 16)
- [[Slack]] — Nassim Taleb; output maximisation vs. optionality; Nomad <50% capacity; cash worth more than cash; drift risk (Letter 15)
- [[Locker_Room_Culture]] — Charlie Munger's term; win-at-any-costs; "schlock begets schlock"; Upton Sinclair; specialisation/diversification as marketing dogma (Letter 17)
- [[Character_as_Moat]] — <5% of firms do what's right; "divine discontent"; internal compass pointing to customer; reinvestment risk reduction (Letter 17)
- [[Interlocking_Advantages]] — Sol Price 1967 memo; "no secret sauce"; one-big-thing vs. many-small-things; framing error; Ole Peters branching probability; Amazon 452 goals (Letter 18)
- [[Inactivity_as_Strategy]] — active decision not to trade; reinvestment risk elimination; UK Top Gun sitting on hands; William James; Pavlovian antidote (Letters 19–20)
- [[Information_as_Food]] — JP Rangaswami analogy; Darwin/Attenborough model; Expensive Tissue Hypothesis; shelf-life hierarchy; near-axiomatic SES insight ranks highest; Ford 1908-1924 chart (Letter 22)
- [[Aggregation_of_Marginal_Gains]] — Dave Brailsford / Team GB Cycling; Costco basis-point cost tracking; Amazon vending machines; Zak's activity centre; good costs vs bad costs; "aggregation of marginal gains" = mechanism behind SES moat (Letter 23)
- [[Non_Monetary_Incentives]] — Dan Ariely; Lego robots (Sisyphus vs rewards, 50%+ more robots); cake mix; origami; Adam Smith vs Marx; founders motivated by meaning not money; Sleep/Zakaria attitude to fees (Letter 24)
- [[Paradox_of_Choice]] — Barry Schwartz; 130 salad dressings; 6m hi-fi combinations; stock markets as the ultimate choice paradox; Ferrari 250 GTO at &#36;52m; always something to make Nomad look weedy; defence: long-term comparison only (Letter 25)
- [[Regulation_and_Industry_Structure]] — 4 regulators; scope up 10×; treats Nomad same as hedge fund; consolidation → fragility (Taleb); "tax short term investing"; detective-regulators vs blanket rules; halcyon days of Farnam St gone; factor in closure (Letter 25)
